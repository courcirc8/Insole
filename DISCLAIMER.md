# Medical & Safety Disclaimer

**This project is not a medical device and is not certified as one.**

`Insole` is research and hobbyist software for reconstructing and generating
3D-printable insole geometry. It has not been evaluated or approved by any
regulatory authority (FDA, EU MDR/CE, Swissmedic, or any other).

By using this software you acknowledge that:

- **No medical claim is made.** Nothing produced by this pipeline is a
  diagnosis, a treatment, or a prescription. The anatomical terms used in the
  code (arch support, heel posting, metatarsal pad, retro-capital bar,
  met-head relief) describe *geometry*, not clinical indications.
- **Orthotics can cause harm.** An incorrectly shaped, positioned, or overly
  aggressive insole can cause pain, skin breakdown, altered gait, falls, or
  joint injury. Risk is substantially higher for people with diabetes,
  peripheral neuropathy, peripheral vascular disease, or reduced sensation,
  where an unnoticed pressure point can progress to ulceration.
- **A qualified clinician must validate any orthotic** (podiatrist,
  orthopedist, or certified orthotist) before it is worn, and especially
  before prolonged or load-bearing use.
- **Printed materials are not certified for skin contact.** Filament choice,
  print hygiene, and post-processing are the user's responsibility.
- **The software is provided "as is"**, without warranty of any kind, under
  the terms of the [MIT License](LICENSE). The authors accept no liability
  for any injury, loss, or damage arising from its use.

The bundled presets (for example `presets/griffes_orteils.yaml`) are
illustrative geometry examples, not clinical recommendations for any
condition or any individual.

If you experience pain, numbness, discoloration, or skin damage while using a
printed insole, stop using it and consult a healthcare professional.
